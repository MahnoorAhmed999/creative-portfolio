Dear reader,

I’m Mahnoor, and online I’m Mano. I study at FAST-NUCES Islamabad and I spend a lot of my time making things look good for the people around me.

I design for societies, run social media, edit videos and help pull off events. I like bold colors, handmade textures and posts that feel like a person made them.

This page is my scrapbook of that work. Flip through, steal a color, say hi.

What I make

Posters and graphics

Event posters, announcements and branding kits, mostly built in Canva.

Social media

Planning, posting and keeping accounts alive. I keep up with trends so feeds never feel stale.

Video editing

Short reels and event recaps cut to feel like the day did.

Events and teams

Sponsorship outreach, campus campaigns and leading creative teams through busy seasons.
